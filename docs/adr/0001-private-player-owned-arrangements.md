# Keep player-owned arrangements private

FretCoach will begin with private player-owned arrangements and no public tab catalogue. This keeps the product focused on diagnosis and mastery rather than competing for licensed music, while avoiding the copyright, moderation, and takedown operations that public user-uploaded tabs would require. A future public catalogue requires a separate licensed-content decision.

## Consequences

The import flow must help players bring a lawfully obtained supported file, and pilot research must test whether that friction is acceptable. Public-tab upload, search, sharing rewards, and discovery are out of scope.
