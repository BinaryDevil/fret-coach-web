# Make performance evidence authoritative before AI coaching

FretCoach will initially assess only calibrated, assessable single-note passages using product-defined pitch and onset-timing criteria. The accuracy rating must expose its pitch, timing, and input-confidence evidence; AI may explain that evidence and propose an intervention hypothesis, but may not replace or override measurement.

## Consequences

Chords, strumming, overlapping arpeggios, expressive techniques, and unsupported score timing remain outside the initial assessment claim. An unassessable input condition produces no rating, and player-selected practice-assist strictness cannot alter mastery.
